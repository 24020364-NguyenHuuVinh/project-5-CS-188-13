Pacman is a classic arcade game developed by Namco in 1980. 
In the game, Pacman navigates a maze to consume all pellets while avoiding four colored ghosts: Blinky, Pinky, Inky, and Clyde. 
When Pacman eats a power pellet, the ghosts turn blue, allowing Pacman to eat them for extra points. 
Artificial intelligence techniques like search algorithms, Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, and neural networks can be used to control Pacman autonomously.
Pacman searches for the optimal path to clear the maze safely and achieve the highest score.
